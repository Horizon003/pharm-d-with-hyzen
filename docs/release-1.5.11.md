# PHARM D WITH HYZEN 1.5.11 — Study Room & Ranking

- First-visit ranking setup asks for a display name and an explicit public/private choice. Students can save preferences while their professional year awaits administrator review; they cannot self-verify or invent scores.
- Redesigned professional ranking, account choice, login, welcome notices and announcements in the existing pastel/Outfit system. Email/password fields use readable text and accessible controls.
- Generated clay illustrations for lectures, documents without extensions, subjects, announcements, login and ranking.
- YouTube videos and playlists open in the app’s lecture room. Embedding restrictions from video owners still apply.
- Existing study tracking, reminders, rewards, documents, downloads, admin import/export and production signing are retained.

Review prerelease. Physical Infinix Note 8, real camera/Drive and YouTube playback on a physical device require verification. Emulator profile evidence does not establish 60 FPS on physical hardware.
