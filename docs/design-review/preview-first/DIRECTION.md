before they are used. No production Flutter source, assets or release are changed
at this stage.

`.agents/skills/ui-ux-pro-max`. Its local design-system, Flutter and compact-label
searches were executed. The first education match targeted children (Baloo/Comic

academic/document/account/admin feature. No automatic background animation.

rows, a small purpose-built study panda and black pill navigation.

stronger asymmetric hierarchy, the same typography and compact controls.
