A dedicated control channel acquires a cancellable export lease and handles Cancel immediately outside the queued PDF worker. Cancellation checks occur before draft-page rendering, between PDF image pages and before/after PDF writing. Current bitmap/PDFBox work finishes to its next check; the UI says Stopping export rather than claiming immediate interruption. Partial PDF output and temporary rendered scan pages are cleaned on normal cancellation, while originals, current draft and removed-page Undo are retained.

Completion and cancellation are arbitrated under the same synchronized lease: a successful cancel cannot deliver its finished PDF into history; a cancel arriving after successful completion returns false and the existing save/history/archive/clear sequence proceeds. The UI hides cancellation during finalization. Errors/cancellation never clear the draft. Existing busy/back guards remain in effect. Older native builds without the new control channel use the legacy export path without exposing a false Cancel capability.
